# 3. Farming for Projects

Note that New Farms are implemented at each Farm Rebalance which are bi-weekly.

There are 2 Options for Farming on Minswap for other Projects. Each Option has different requirements:

1. **Own Token Farm:** for example, if your project token is $XZY, you can enable $XZY/$ADA Liquidity Providers to Farm $XZY. There are **no requirements** to enable this type of Farm. However, due to the high demand to implement this kind of farm, a one-time fixed Fee of 500 ADA is required.\
   \
   The steps therefore to get an Own Token Farm are:\
   \
   **1)** Decide on the amount of tokens you want to put on the farm program and for how long (for example, 0.5% of the total supply for 6 months).\
   \
   **2)** Send the one-time fixed Fee of 500 ADA to the following address: $minswap\
   \
   **3)** Drop a message on the Minswap [Discord](https://discord.gg/ZjB8ZBhkbm) and you will be assisted in the final step.
2. **MIN Farm (Farm Rebalance):** MIN rewards are set at every Farm Rebalance. Minswap reviews all pools with the same rules and decides which pools receive MIN and how much.\
   \
   **Who can take part**

   * Every pool that already has a MIN farm, and every Minswap V2 or Stable pool with enough liquidity, even without a farm.
   * The pool needs a minimum level of TVL, trading volume over the last 30 days, and at least 30 days of trading history.
   * A **new** MIN farm also needs a higher TVL and **both tokens verified** (see [Token Listing & Verification](token-listing-and-verification.md)).

   **What decides the MIN rewards**

   * **Trading activity:** the volume and the trading fees the pool makes. This is the main metric. Recent activity counts most, and longer periods also count, so one busy week or a busy past alone is not enough.
   * **Liquidity depth:** the pool's TVL, but only liquidity that traders actually use.
   * **Fee APR:** pools whose LPs already earn high fees receive less extra MIN.
   * **Project's own rewards:** projects that also reward their farmers with their own token (Own Token Farm) can receive more MIN.

   **How rewards change**

   * Changes are gradual: a farm can lose only part of its rewards in one rebalance.
   * A farm that no longer meets the rules above stops receiving MIN.

   The current MIN points of every farm and the change at each rebalance are shown on the [Yield Dashboard](https://minswap.org/analytics/yield-dashboard).
